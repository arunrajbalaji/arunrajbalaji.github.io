The opening of a page: an accent bar, the headline and one sentence beneath it.

- Provide one headline and one lede sentence. On the homepage both are fixed copy.
- The headline is regular weight. Do not make it semibold, bold or larger than `display-size`; that weight and size is what made an earlier version read as a sales pitch.
- Keep the headline to two lines on desktop with `measure-display`.
- Use one Hero per page, and only on pages that open with a statement. List pages such as Publications start with a plain page title instead.
- The accent bar is the only use of `accent` in this region.
