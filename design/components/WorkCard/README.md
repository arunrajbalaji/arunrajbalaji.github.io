A bordered panel for one piece of work: a figure on grid paper, a title, a short description and a reference line.

- Provide a figure, a title that links to the case study, one paragraph, and a metadata line.
- Write the title to name the method or the result, not only the device. Leave off the trailing full stop.
- For published work the metadata line is the exact reference, linked to its DOI: journal, volume, article number, year. For other work it is the organisation and dates as plain text.
- Every card in a row carries a figure, so no card looks lighter than its neighbours. Where no real figure can be shown, use a plain schematic.
- The figure in this preview is a sketch, not data. On the site, use the real published figure, sized for the card and placed on `plate` in both themes.
- Cards sit three across in the full content column and stack on a phone. The metadata line pins to the bottom, so the lines align across a row.
- Technical terms are welcome here. This is where a reader has chosen to look closer.
