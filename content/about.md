Heading: About

I'm Raj Balaji-Wright, a computational scientist in Cambridge, Massachusetts. I build simulation software for nonlinear, multi-scale systems, and I work across the whole path: the model on paper, the numerical method, and the software people run.

Since 2025 I have been at Ark Biotech, where I'm a Senior Modeler building bioprocess simulation software.

Before Ark, I spent my Ph.D. at Stanford on theory and computation: models and solvers for CO2 electroreduction, redox flow batteries, capacitive deionization and electrodialysis. I had started in experiments. As an undergraduate at Princeton I built and tested drag-reducing surfaces and microfluidic devices, which taught me what a model has to answer to.

The domains have changed and the work has not. It is still a matter of separating what matters from what doesn't, then making the result fast and robust enough to use.

## Outside work

I'm an amateur endurance runner and a lifelong musician. I play jazz piano, percussion and guitar, and I've performed in an orchestra and at a wedding gig. I'm always looking for people to jam with, and I go to live shows whenever I can: jazz, rock and folk.

From 2017 to 2022 I helped lead the Stanford chapter of Future Advancers of Science and Technology (FAST), which pairs graduate student mentors with high school students for hands-on research projects. I served as its president, among other roles, and helped it become an independent nonprofit.
